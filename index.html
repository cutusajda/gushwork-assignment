Frequently Asked Questions

What is the purpose of a laser cutter for sheet metal?
It is designed to cut various types of sheet metal with precision, allowing for intricate designs and shapes that are essential in manufacturing processes.
What are the benefits of using aluminum tubing in manufacturing?
Aluminum tubing offers lightweight properties, excellent corrosion resistance, and a high strength-to-weight ratio — ideal for technical applications.
How is aluminum tubing produced?
Aluminum tubing is produced through extrusion: heated billets are pushed through a shaped die, then drawn to the final dimensions.
What are the common applications of aluminum tubing?
Structural frameworks, fluid transport, automotive, aerospace, and construction — wherever a light, strong, corrosion-resistant material is needed.
Can aluminum tubing be customized?
Yes — diameter, length, pressure rating, and end-connection can all be tailored to specific project requirements.

Advanced HDPE Pipe Manufacturing Process

Our state-of-the-art extrusion technology ensures consistent quality, optimal material properties, and dimensional accuracy in every pipe we manufacture.

Step 1/8: Raw Material

High-Grade Raw Material Selection

Vacuum sizing tanks ensure precise outer diameter while internal pressure maintains perfect roundness and wall thickness uniformity.

  • PE100 grade material
  • Optimal molecular weight distribution
Step 2/8: Extrusion

Precision Extrusion Process

Advanced extruders process PE100 raw material at controlled temperatures to ensure homogeneous melt and consistent pipe wall thickness throughout the production run.

  • Controlled temperature profiles
  • Uniform melt flow distribution
  • Continuous monitoring systems
Step 3/8: Cooling

Controlled Cooling System

Water-based cooling tanks rapidly cool the extruded pipe while maintaining dimensional accuracy and preventing warping or deformation.

  • Precision water cooling
  • Temperature-controlled baths
  • Stress-free solidification
Step 4/8: Sizing

Vacuum Sizing

Vacuum sizing tanks ensure precise outer diameter while internal pressure maintains perfect roundness and wall thickness uniformity for every pipe produced.

  • Accurate outer diameter control
  • Consistent wall thickness
Step 5/8: Quality Control

Rigorous Quality Control

Each pipe undergoes hydrostatic pressure tests, dimensional checks, and material property verification before leaving our facility.

  • Hydrostatic pressure testing
  • Visual and dimensional inspection
Step 6/8: Marking

Indelible Marking

Every pipe is clearly marked with size, pressure rating, standard compliance, and batch number for complete traceability.

  • Standard-compliant marking
  • Full batch traceability
Step 7/8: Cutting

Precision Cutting

Automated cutting systems produce clean, square pipe ends ensuring perfect butt fusion and electrofusion joins on site.

  • Square cut pipe ends
  • Custom length options
Step 8/8: Packaging

Safe Packaging & Dispatch

Pipes are bundled, strapped, and labelled before dispatch to ensure they arrive undamaged and installation-ready.

  • Protective bundling
  • Nationwide delivery network
